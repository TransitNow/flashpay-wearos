Privacy Policy — Flashpay

Last updated 9 August 2026

Flashpay is a Wear OS app that turns your watch screen into a flashlight and can open your wallet app from a hardware button. This policy covers the Flashpay app, package com.jsyntax.nowtap.

Data we collect

None. Flashpay does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no accounts.

Network access

Flashpay does not request the internet permission and cannot make network connections. Nothing leaves your watch because there is no way for it to do so.

Permissions

Flashpay requests one permission, VIBRATE, used only to produce the short haptic tap that confirms the shortcut fired.

Sensors

Flashpay reads the watch's ambient light sensor once, at the moment you press the button, purely to decide whether to open the flashlight or your wallet app. The reading is used immediately and then discarded. It is never stored and never transmitted.

Data stored on your watch

Flashpay saves your preferences — flashlight colour, brightness step, timer length, and the time of your last button press so a quick second press can switch modes — in Android's private app storage on the watch. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Other apps

Flashpay can ask Android to open Google Wallet, exactly as tapping that app's icon would. Flashpay does not process payments, access cards, or receive any information back from it. Anything you do inside Google Wallet is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not this one. Flashpay is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Children

Flashpay is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
